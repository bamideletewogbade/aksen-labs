Close today's entry in `Docs/Daily-Log.md`.

## Ask, if it was not said

For each of today's three: done or not. One question, all three at once. Do not ask about anything else.

If `$ARGUMENTS` already says what happened, use it and ask nothing.

## Record

- Mark each of the three `done` or `skipped` in today's `Docs/Daily-Log.md` entry.
- Tick the completed items in `Docs/Queue.md`.
- **The streak increments only if SELL was done.** A day of building is not a day. Say this plainly when it happens: "Three build items done, streak still N. The SELL lane is the one that counts."
- If an item was skipped for the third day running, flag it for splitting at `/weekly`.
- If a conversation with an owner happened, increment the scoreboard and ask for one line of what they actually said, in their words, for `Docs/Owner-Conversations.md`. That sentence is worth more than the rest of the day.
- If money was invoiced, update the scoreboard and, if it relates to a revenue ledger item, move that item along in `Docs/Revenue-Ledger.md`.

## Then stop

One line of acknowledgement at most. No summary of the day, no praise, no plan for tomorrow. `/today` does tomorrow.

If the day was a zero, say: "Nothing today. Floor tomorrow is one message." Nothing else. Do not soften it and do not pile on.
