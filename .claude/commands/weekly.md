The weekly review from `Docs/Business-OS.md`. Fifteen minutes, and it is allowed to deliver bad news.

## Read

`Docs/Daily-Log.md` (this week's entries), `Docs/Queue.md`, `Docs/Revenue-Ledger.md`, `Docs/Aksen-Operating-Brief.md` sections 4 and 6.

## 1. The two numbers

Count them from the log. Do not adjust, do not explain, do not average. Write them into the scoreboard in `Docs/Daily-Log.md`.

If both are zero, increment the zero-week counter. **At three, open the review with it and say plainly that the strategy is wrong and building will not fix it.** Then ask one question: what would have to be true for a business owner to pay this month?

## 2. Carried items

Anything unticked for three days running: split it into something that fits in 25 minutes, or kill it. An item that has been avoided three times is either too big or not actually wanted. Say which you think it is.

## 3. Move the money

Walk `Docs/Revenue-Ledger.md`. For each item not Parked or Killed:

- Did anything happen this week that earns it a stage move? Nothing moves out of politeness or effort. Noticed → Sized needs a written mechanism and a cost to test. Sized → Testing needs a test actually started and an exit criterion written **before** it starts. Testing → Earning needs money to have arrived.
- Is any exit-criterion date within seven days, or passed? A passed date with no result is a failed test. Say so and move it to Killed or back to Sized with a reason.
- Are more than two items in Testing? Something comes out today.
- Did this week produce a new mechanism worth noticing? Add it, at Noticed, with who pays for what and how often.

## 4. Refill the queue

Each lane to at least five items, from the current phase and from ledger next-actions. Every item 25 minutes or less, specific enough to start without thinking.

Move ticked items to the Done section with their date.

## 5. The one question

Ask it in writing and record the answer in the log:

**What did a real business owner tell me this week that I did not already believe?**

If the answer is nothing, next week's SELL lane is conversations only. No exceptions, and say so.

## 6. Phase check

Is the current phase's exit test passed? If yes, move to the next phase, update section 6 of the operating brief, add a dated line to its decision log, and rewrite the BUILD lane from the new phase. If no, say what is left.

## Output

Short. The two numbers first, any bad news second, what changed third. No congratulation.
