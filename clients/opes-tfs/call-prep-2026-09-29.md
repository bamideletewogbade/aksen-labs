## The frame for the whole call

Every business has three problems, in this order:

1. **Leads.** Not enough people know about it, or can find it when they look.
2. **Customers.** Not enough of the people who ask end up buying.
3. **Profit.** Not enough of each sale is kept, and not enough buyers come back.

Do not pitch all three. Find out which one hurts her most, and offer one small fix for that one.

## Call flow, about 20 minutes

1. **Warm up (2 min).** Congratulate the interior move. Name something specific: the vase posts, the site-visit reel.
2. **Three questions, then listen (8 min).** The three from the table above, in order. Write down her exact words.
3. **The real question (2 min).** "Of those three, which one would you fix first if you could?" Then stop talking.
4. **Offer (5 min).** One starter, in one breath: what she gets, the price, how long. For example: "For the enquiries, I can set up your WhatsApp catalogue, quick replies and a follow-up list in one week for N80,000."
5. **Ask and stop talking.** "Shall I start on Monday?"
6. **Close (2 min).** Get her email address, so she goes into the Aksen workspace and the recap goes out through it. Agree a day for her decision.
