# Discovery questions for Ope

For the first real conversation. The aim is to find one workflow worth fixing and to size it honestly, not to pitch a website.

Everything in `discovery-brief.md` is guesswork from public profiles. These questions are what turn it into fact.

## Where the work actually gets stuck

1. How many messages land on the WhatsApp number in a normal week, and who answers them?
2. What do people ask most often, before they ask for a price?
3. How long does a first reply usually take, and what happens to messages that arrive at night or over a weekend?
4. Do enquiries ever get lost or answered twice? Roughly how often?
5. When somebody asks for a price, what do you need to know before you can give one? Size, room, finish, quantity, delivery address?

## How an order runs today

6. Walk me through one recent order from first message to delivery.
7. Where is the order written down after the chat? Notebook, spreadsheet, someone's memory?
8. How does payment settle, and at what point in the conversation?
9. How is delivery arranged, and how far does nationwide really reach?
10. What happens with a custom or commissioned piece that is different from a stock item?

## The three brands

11. Are @theframe_shop, @decors_store and @tfs_gallery one business or three, in your head and in your books?
12. Do the same customers buy across them, or are they genuinely different people?
13. Is @theframeshop.ng yours?
14. If somebody says the name of your business out loud, which of the three do you want them to say?

## The two customer types

15. How much of the money comes from hotels, short-lets and interior designers, versus individual buyers?
16. Which of the two would you rather have more of next year?
17. For hospitality work, how does that usually start? Referral, DM, site visit?

## What has already been tried

18. Has there ever been a website, or a shop on Jiji or anywhere else? What happened to it?
19. Do you run ads, or is it all organic on Instagram?
20. What is the one thing about running this that most makes you want to throw your phone?

## Commercial reality

21. Who else touches the business day to day, and would they use anything we build?
22. Is there a budget in your head already, or are we starting from zero?
23. What would have to be true in three months for you to say this was worth doing?

## What not to do in this conversation

- Do not quote a price. There is not enough information yet, and Aksen's own indicative pricing is in GHS for a Ghanaian market.
- Do not promise a website. The website may not be the first thing worth building.
- Do not describe anything Aksen has not actually built and run.
- Do not lean on the school connection to close. Use it to get an honest answer to question 20.
