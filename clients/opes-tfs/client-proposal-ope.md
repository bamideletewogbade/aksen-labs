EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A connected digital platform for your business

Ope, we have undertaken an extensive review of the businesses we could identify through your public Instagram presence, including @theframe_shop, @tfs_gallery and @decors_store. Your profiles present a complementary range of wall framing, bespoke wall decor and home accessories, alongside installation work for homes and hospitality spaces. This proposal reflects that breadth and the distinct needs of customers buying individual pieces and those commissioning a complete project.

Aksen Labs proposes a shared digital platform that presents these offerings clearly, supports online purchases and project enquiries, and gives your team a central place to manage products, orders and customer information. The proposed structure brings the brands together under The Frame Shop, with the presentation and relationship between them agreed with you during design.

An AI agent will support customer service and everyday sales tasks using your approved catalogue, prices and business policies. Customers can access the agent through the website and WhatsApp, while your team uses the same business information to support enquiries and prepare responses. Bespoke pricing, project commitments and exceptions remain with your team.

What the engagement delivers

A mobile-friendly storefront for frames, art and decor, with product details and secure online checkout.

A projects portfolio and structured enquiry process for bespoke work and installations.

An AI service connected to approved business information, with website and WhatsApp access and clear handover to staff.

An admin dashboard, internal AI assistance and reporting to support the daily operation.

Investment

The complete implementation is ₦250,000. A recurring fee of ₦30,000 per month covers the AI subscriptions that power the included AI services, beginning at launch. Implementation includes staff training, handover and a 30-day post-launch warranty.

SCOPE OF WORK

Digital commerce and business operations

Storefront and projects portfolio

The retail shop will organise your approved catalogue by product category, with available sizes, finishes, prices and delivery information. Customers will be able to select products and pay securely through Paystack. A separate projects area will present selected installations and collect the project type, location, dimensions and timeline needed for your team to assess an enquiry.

AI customer service and sales assistance

The AI agent will answer routine questions using approved product and policy information, guide customers to suitable catalogue options, collect order details and share secure payment links. Website chat and WhatsApp will serve as interfaces to this shared service. Custom requests, complaints and questions outside the approved information will be passed to staff with the relevant context.

Business rules and staff oversight

Prices and availability will come from the maintained catalogue. Payment status will be confirmed through the payment integration before an order is marked as paid. Your team will approve bespoke quotations, discounts and delivery commitments that fall outside the agreed rules.

Team tools and reporting

A self-service dashboard will allow your team to maintain products and availability, review orders and enquiries, and update project photographs. An internal AI assistant will help staff find catalogue information and draft responses or quotations for review. Reporting will show recorded orders, sales and enquiry activity to support follow-up and performance reviews.

Configuration and handover

Aksen Labs will configure the platform and integrations, test purchase and enquiry journeys, and train Ope and designated team members. Handover includes account access and operating guidance. Catalogue content, policies, brand presentation and escalation contacts will be agreed before launch.
