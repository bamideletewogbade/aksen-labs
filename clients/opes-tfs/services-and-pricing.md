# Services and pricing: Nigeria & Aksen Labs Standard Model

Updated September 2026. What Aksen sells, what it costs, and how it is priced for the Nigerian market against both local Lagos comparables and global agency benchmarks.

## 1. Market Research: Global vs. Local Nigerian Benchmarks (2025–2026)

Before setting or reducing prices, we conducted a rigorous comparative analysis of what the market charges for custom e-commerce builds, AI customer agents, and WhatsApp integrations.

### Global Benchmarks (US, UK, Western Europe & Remote Agencies)

| Service Layer | Market Range (USD) | Scope & Industry Norms |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom E-Commerce Build** | $4,000 – $25,000 | Shopify Plus, WooCommerce, or custom Next.js stack with payment gateways, inventory management, and custom UX. |
| **Custom AI WhatsApp / Web Agent** | $3,500 – $15,000 | LLM-powered (OpenAI/Anthropic), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over product catalogues, cart links, API-driven lead routing. |
| **Combined E-Commerce + AI Agent** | $8,000 – $35,000 | Full-stack agency build integrating catalog, payment checkout, and live automated concierge across web and messaging. |
| **Monthly Managed Care & Retainer** | $250 – $1,200 / mo | Cloud hosting, model inference API allowance, prompt tuning, uptime monitoring, bug fixes, and minor content updates. |

*Takeaway:* Globally, action-taking AI agents that connect directly to e-commerce and generate payment links command premium custom development fees and substantial ongoing software retainers.

### Local Nigerian Benchmarks (Lagos Market, 2025–2026)

Lagos has a vibrant but price-sensitive digital agency and freelance market. The local benchmarks break down as follows:

| Service Category | Lagos Market Price (NGN) | Notes & Realities |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard Business Website** (5–8 pages) | ₦250,000 – ₦450,000 | Typical WordPress/Elementor agency build. No e-commerce, no conversational AI. |
| **Standard E-Commerce Website** | ₦450,000 – ₦850,000 | WooCommerce or Shopify with Paystack / Flutterwave integration, product catalogue, and mobile-first checkout. |
| **Custom Dual-Journey / Advanced E-Commerce** | ₦800,000 – ₦2,000,000 | Custom portals combining retail checkout (B2C) and bespoke project quotation/portfolio (B2B/Hospitality). |
| **WhatsApp AI Bot / Automation (Agency Setup)** | ₦350,000 – ₦1,200,000 | Ranging from no-code bot setups (ManyChat/Wati) to custom LLM agents hooked into product databases. |
| **Bundled E-Commerce + Custom AI WhatsApp Agent** | ₦900,000 – ₦2,500,000 | Top-tier Lagos digital agencies (eBrand Promotion, Sizzle Digital, Edens Digital) delivering end-to-end builds. |
| **Monthly Retainer & Technical Support** | ₦35,000 – ₦100,000 / mo | Standard maintenance for SMEs is ₦30,000–₦50,000/mo; active stores pay ₦70,000–₦120,000/mo. |

#### Crucial Local Context: Staffing Equivalence in Lagos
- A junior sales assistant or in-store customer support rep in Lagos earns approximately **₦50,000 to ₦80,000 per month**.
- If Aksen's monthly retainer was set at a flat **₦75,000 to ₦120,000/month**, a small business owner compares that directly to the salary of an in-person employee.
- **The Strategic Correction:** Reducing the entry maintenance retainer to **₦35,000 – ₦45,000/month** positions the 24/7 AI system as *cheaper than half the salary of one junior employee*, while working 24/7, never sleeping, answering 11 PM queries, and never missing an order.

#### WhatsApp Business API Cost Reality (Meta Pass-Through)
- Meta charges per-message for the official WhatsApp Business Platform:
  - **Service/Utility messages** (customer replies within the 24-hour customer service window): ~$0.0101 (approx. **₦14 – ₦16** per message).
  - **Marketing templates** (outbound broadcasts): ~$0.0516 – $0.062 (approx. **₦75 – ₦90** per message).
- Because The Frame Shop is primarily responding to incoming customer enquiries rather than blasting cold messages, the actual Meta API cost is minimal (under ₦15,000/mo for standard volume).

## 3. Reviewed & Reduced Pricing for Ope (The Frame Shop)

Ope is Aksen Labs' **Founding Client / Flagship Case Study** in Nigeria. 

### Why We Reduced the Prices
1. **The Previous Setup Rate (₦525,000 founding / ₦1,150,000 standard):** While defensible against agency rates, ₦525,000 upfront creates unnecessary friction for an initial conversation with a warm intro.
2. **The Previous Monthly Retainer (₦75,000/mo flat):** ₦75,000 every month is ₦900,000/year. For a home decor store, that feels like paying a second manager. Reducing the retainer to **₦35,000/mo** makes it an obvious "yes" that stays subscribed for years.
3. **The Frame Unit Economics:** Ope sells a single 36x70 inch frame for **₦85,000**. 
   - At **₦420,000** for the Recommended Tier, **the entire build is paid off by selling just 5 frames.**
   - Framing this mathematically in front of her turns the cost from an expense into an immediate positive return.

### The Reviewed Founding Partner Rates for Ope

| Package | Standard Agency Price | **Founding Partner Rate (Reduced)** | What Ope Pays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01. Shopfront** | ₦650,000 | **₦280,000** | ₦140,000 to start / ₦140,000 on launch |
| **02. Shopfront + Projects (Recommended)** | ₦950,000 | **₦420,000** | ₦210,000 to start / ₦210,000 on launch |
| **03. Whole Operation** | ₦1,250,000 | **₦580,000** | ₦290,000 to start / ₦290,000 on launch |

### The Founding Partner Exchange
The reduced rate is a **Founding Partner Exchange**, not an arbitrary discount:
1. Permission to feature The Frame Shop publicly as a flagship case study.
2. Sharing before-and-after business metrics (e.g., number of captured orders, response time improvements).
3. Two warm introductions to other business owners in Lagos upon successful launch.

### Ongoing Care for Ope
- **First 30 Days:** **FREE** (Included in the project build).
- **Subsequent Months:** **₦35,000 / month** (Essential Care tier, covering hosting, SSL, 24/7 AI WhatsApp agent operation, and support).

## 4. Payment & Delivery Terms
- **50% deposit** upon project kickoff.
- **50% upon handover**, strictly defined as: **The website is live on her domain and successfully processing test card transactions via Paystack/Flutterwave.**
- No hidden markups: Third-party domain registration (~₦10,000–₦18,000/yr) and direct payment gateway processing fees (Paystack 1.5% capped at ₦2,000) are paid directly to the providers.
