3. What Is Needed on Your Part on Hostinger (Step-by-Step)

To link your Hostinger account with your live domain and activate professional email, please complete the following steps (or grant us access as described below):

Step 1 · Choose Your Preferred Method

Option A (Fastest & Recommended): Add our team as an Authorized Collaborator in your Hostinger account so our engineers can configure the DNS and mail settings directly for you.

How to do this: Log into Hostinger → Click your profile icon at top right → Account Sharing → Invite Collaborator → Enter our agency email with "Manage Services & Domains" permission.

Step 2 · If You Prefer to Do It Manually
  1. Log into your Hostinger hPanel.
  2. Navigate to Emails → Select efeorganics.com → Create your desired corporate mailboxes (e.g. hello@efeorganics.com, orders@efeorganics.com).
  3. Go to Websites → DNS / Nameservers. Ensure the zone is active and provide our team with the MX records Hostinger generates.
Step 3 · Settle the Pending Avehost Domain Invoice

In your Avehost client portal (where efeorganics.com was purchased), there is a yellow banner indicating an unpaid annual renewal invoice (GH₵200.00). Please log into Avehost and settle this via Mobile Money to prevent the registrar from placing a hold on the domain.
