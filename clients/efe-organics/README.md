## Key Strategic Context

Efe Organics built its initial customer base and product formulation reputation over a decade (EST. 2012). However, their complete commercial catalog was previously hosted exclusively on a third-party reseller (`coloursbay.com`). 

Aksen Labs is transforming Efe into an owned digital powerhouse:
1. **Owned Commerce**: Complete independence from third-party platforms with direct Ghana MoMo checkout.
2. **Relational Inventory**: Inverting flat reseller SKUs into clean product-family architecture (e.g., Lemon Blast across 350ml, 500ml, 1L sizes).
3. **Wholesale Integration**: Segregating raw formulation bulk supplies (e.g., 250kg soap crumble @ GH₵13,750.00) from consumer retail items.
4. **AI-Driven Customer Experience**: Grounded skincare consultation and automated marketing copy generation via Blotato and OpenRouter.
