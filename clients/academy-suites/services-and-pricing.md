## Delivery exclusions

Exclude live inventory integration, payment processing, PMS replacement, migration, other branches, new photography and unrestricted support unless separately investigated and priced. Identify domain, hosting, provider fees and renewal responsibility. Use approved photographs and content.

Before taking a deposit: written deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, revision limits, acceptance checks, payment milestones and a feasible schedule. A deposit commits delivery capacity and costs; do not treat it as personal profit.

Suggested acceptance: approved content on agreed pages, correct branch destinations, responsive layouts, understandable room/event requests, authorised test received by reception, and a short handover. No guarantee of increased bookings.
