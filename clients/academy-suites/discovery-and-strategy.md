## Discovery sequence

1. How do most Old Ife Road guests find you and enquire today?
2. Could you walk me through one recent enquiry, without guest personal details, from the first message to confirmation?
3. What information do staff usually have to ask for again? Does anything need chasing between shifts?
4. Which is more useful to improve now: room enquiries, event enquiries, website information, or follow-up?
5. Who maintains the website and updates rates and availability? Is there an existing support contract?
6. Who would approve this work and its payment? What budget and timing would make it practical?
7. What would need to be true for you to say this work was worth paying for?

Use questions naturally, following their answers. Ask for channel fees, booking contribution and volumes only if they want to assess commercial value. Gross room revenue is not profit; time saved is not automatically cash saved.

## Fit and decision

Strong next step: a named approver, a specific problem they recognise, a feasible scope, access path and a dated quotation discussion. A compliment on the demo alone is not qualification.

If no unmet need or budget exists, record that honestly. If their provider can fix the contact defects, share the observations and establish whether there is any separate work worth buying. Do not keep building on speculative interest.
