## 5. What not to say

These are firm, because saying them costs more than any sale:

- No "we have helped X businesses", no results, no testimonials. We do not have Nigerian clients yet, and the businesses in the images are fictional.
- No "guaranteed", "never miss an order", "replaces your staff".
- No prices that are not in this file, and no discounts. If someone wants cheaper, the answer is a smaller first step, not a lower price.
- AI does not run anyone's business. It prepares; a person decides.
