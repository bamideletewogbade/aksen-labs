## 03 A tighter business profile

**Category:** AI workflow implementation and managed operations.

**One-line description:** We set up and run practical AI workflows that connect your enquiries, business tools and team, so work moves from request to completion with less manual chasing.

**Company profile:** We help growing service businesses organize customer enquiries, prepare follow-up, and move approved work into delivery. We map a specific process, connect the tools and information the team already uses, add AI where it improves the work, and train staff to operate the system. After launch, we monitor failures, maintain approved knowledge and integrations, and review results with the business owner. Each engagement has a defined scope, a named owner and measurable acceptance criteria.

This is proposed positioning. Statements about monitoring and delivery describe the service to establish; do not present them as existing client results until fulfilled.

**Public offer structure:** Assess -> Implement -> Operate. Explain the business job before explaining orchestration. Keep websites, creative production and broad transformation available as separately scoped supporting services; make the core buying journey about one operational problem.

| Initial test cohort | First job | Proof to seek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property agencies with regular enquiries and maintained listings | Enquiry qualification, approved answers and viewing handoff | Follow-up completion, qualified viewing requests, response time |
| Commercial cleaning and maintenance firms handling repeated quotes | Enquiry intake, quote preparation and approved job handoff | Quote turnaround, administrative minutes per enquiry, handoff completeness |

The second cohort is a test hypothesis informed by the local Youth Alive service-work prototype, not a claim about a paying client or proven segment demand. Prioritize whichever cohort provides accessible records, an accountable buyer and paid commitment. Use REAC to verify property practitioners where relevant. [REAC Public Registry; undated, checked 4 September 2026](https://reac.gov.gh/public-registry-search/).

Treat 10-100 employees as a prospecting aid. More important filters are recurring work volume, demonstrable delay or error, a maintained information source, ability to approve a purchase, and enough economic benefit to cover implementation and care. A five-person firm can qualify; a larger firm with no owner or clean process can fail.

## 06 Price for delivery effort and recurring value

**Illustrative economics only:** use GHS 120/hour as a placeholder loaded delivery cost until actual costs are known. Founder time is a cost even when no salary is drawn. Contribution below excludes sales time, general overhead and tax; it is not take-home profit.

| Scenario | Calculation | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GHS 9,000 pilot, controlled scope | 36 total hours x GHS 120 + GHS 300 incidentals = GHS 4,620 | GHS 4,380 / 48.7% |
| Same pilot with rework | 60 hours x GHS 120 + GHS 300 = GHS 7,500 | GHS 1,500 / 16.7% |
| GHS 3,000 monthly care | 8 hours x GHS 120 + GHS 300 direct costs = GHS 1,260 | GHS 1,740 / 58.0% |
| Same care with heavy support | 20 hours x GHS 120 + GHS 300 = GHS 2,700 | GHS 300 / 10.0% |

**Cost floor:** estimated delivery cost / (1 - chosen contribution margin). At GHS 4,620 cost and a proposed 50% contribution target, the floor is GHS 9,240. The GHS 9,000 pilot is a controlled validation price slightly below that target; use GHS 9,500 or more if the same cost estimate must meet 50%. Decline or reduce scope when the buyer's value cannot support the floor.

Proposed terms: assessment paid upfront; pilot 50% deposit, 30% on acceptance testing and 20% at handover; care monthly in advance from the agreed start date. Defects in the agreed build remain covered by the defect period; care adds monitoring and improvement. Do not bill twice for the same obligation. Credits or discounts must be written and included in the margin calculation.

**Customer value test:** monthly gross benefit minus care and usage costs must be positive. If a GHS 12,000 setup produces a verified GHS 5,000/month gross benefit, with GHS 3,000 care and GHS 500 usage, simple payback is eight months. A proposed six-month screening target would reject or reshape that deal. These numbers illustrate the method only.

Use recovered gross profit rather than sales revenue. Time saved is released capacity until reduced expenditure or productive redeployment is demonstrated. Ask why ongoing care is needed: changing knowledge, integration failures, exceptions, staff changes and performance reviews can justify it; a stable one-off automation may only need occasional support.

## 07 Technology costs and delivery boundaries

Keep client-owned business accounts and credentials wherever practical, with delegated access for implementation. This supports continuity at handover and makes external usage visible. Preserve the current Postgres and application investment where it passes verification; introduce a workflow tool only when it solves a specific integration need.

n8n lists Starter at EUR 20/month billed annually for 2,500 workflow executions, and Pro at EUR 50/month billed annually for 10,000. These imply EUR 240 and EUR 600 annual plan charges before applicable taxes, not monthly cancel-anytime commitments. Executions are workflow runs, not conversations. A five-minute schedule fires 8,640 times in a 30-day month. [n8n pricing; checked 4 September 2026](https://n8n.io/pricing/).

n8n's official guidance says hosting client workflows and credentials in an agency's own instance requires Enterprise licensing. Consulting on client-owned internal instances does not require a commercial licence on the consultant's part; client requirements still apply. Meta distinguishes destination markets and message categories; service messages within the user-initiated 24-hour service window are free at Meta's layer. Exact Ghana paid-template rates were not verified. Twilio, as one possible provider, lists USD 0.005 per inbound or outbound WhatsApp message plus applicable Meta charges. An assumed 12,000 messages would therefore cost USD 60 in Twilio handling fees alone. [Meta platform pricing](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/) and [Twilio WhatsApp pricing](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/whatsapp/pricing), checked 4 September 2026.

**Before quoting usage:** record channel volume, executions, tokens, selected model, retry allowance, storage, provider fees, exchange rate at billing, taxes and a spending cap. Keep USD/EUR inputs in their original currencies until an actual conversion quote is available. No universal per-client infrastructure budget is claimed here.

Verify model identifiers in the live provider catalogue and run representative tasks before deployment. The project's hard-coded model names are not proof of availability. Use a stable event log and small, replaceable tools; Anthropic's April 2026 architecture separates session history, model execution and tool environments. ## 08 Run the company with its own method

**Internal client zero:** the founder's enquiry-to-proposal-to-delivery process. Define the manual process first, record a baseline, then introduce assistance. A company with little inbound volume should not manufacture activity to make an automation look useful.

| Workflow | AI assistance | Rules and founder responsibility | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prospect research | Summarize verified public business information and prepare a relevant brief | Record sources; founder selects fit and sends outreach | Qualified conversations per researched firm |
| Enquiry intake | Extract need, missing information and draft next step | Create one record; owner and due date required; founder approves reply | Missing fields, response time, duplicate rate |
| Discovery to proposal | Summarize notes, map process and draft scope | Founder verifies baseline, effort, price and terms before sending | Proposal preparation time and correction count |
| Deposit to delivery | Draft checklist, tasks and weekly status | Confirm payment from an authoritative record; approve scope and launch | Actual hours, acceptance results, delivery date |
| Support and review | Classify incidents and draft monthly results | Human handles commitments; review failures and costs | Incidents, recovery time, cost and renewal |
| Finance and knowledge | Categorize receipts and suggest knowledge updates | Reconcile accounts; approve tax treatment and factual changes | Unreconciled items and source freshness |

These are proposed capabilities, not existing integrations. Initially a person can execute approved actions while the system records them. External outreach, proposals and publication remain founder-approved; no messages were sent during this research.

**First internal workflow contract:** trigger = submitted enquiry or manually entered prospect; required fields = source, company, contact basis, observed need, owner, next action and due date; steps = validate -> deduplicate -> save -> draft -> review -> act -> record outcome. On failure, retain the request, alert the owner and permit a safe retry. A request ID should prevent duplicate external actions.

**Proposed internal acceptance gate:** 30 labelled test cases covering missing data, duplicates, unclear requests, unauthorised actions and provider/database failures; no unauthorized sends; no lost accepted requests; duplicate attempts do not duplicate actions. Then run a 14-day internal pilot with every real case reviewed. Test fixtures prove behavior, not customer demand; report real-case counts separately.

The baseline and comparison should track manual minutes, corrections and completion, not just generated text. Anthropic's simpler-workflow guidance supports adding autonomy only when complexity improves measured results. [Building effective agents; 19 December 2024, current page notes newer tooling](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents).

## 09 What to reuse and what to strengthen

The existing platform is an asset to refine. This was a targeted source-code review, not a penetration test, build verification or audit of deployed configuration.

| Observation in current code | Business consequence | Next implementation step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opportunity intake stores a record and an audit event | A useful foundation for the sales workflow exists | Add consistent owner, due date and measurable stage history |
| Conversation updates overwrite a summary; no message-event table appears in the reviewed schema | A summary alone cannot reconstruct the customer interaction | Store appropriately scoped events with agreed retention |
| Agent logging catches and suppresses database errors | A successful response can be missing from the operational record | Surface logging failures and queue durable writes for important events |
| Admin API routes check an email allowlist only when it is populated | An empty configuration can admit any authenticated user at these routes | Deny by default when authorization configuration is missing; test API access directly |
| Approval endpoint updates status and audit record | An approval screen alone does not guarantee controlled execution | Bind approval to exact action/version and execute once after permission checks |
| Model wrapper returns answer/model but no token-usage costs | Cost fields in the schema do not establish actual cost tracking | Capture actual provider usage, price version and cost; reconcile invoices |

Source files reviewed: platform/db/schema.ts; lib/conversations.ts; lib/agent-runs.ts; lib/openrouter.ts; app/api/opportunities/route.ts; app/api/admin/approvals/route.ts; app/api/admin/projects/route.ts. These paths are relative to the Aksen-Labs project. No live vulnerability or actual data exposure is asserted; configuration and runtime behavior were not inspected.

**Build order:** access controls and event integrity -> one enquiry workflow -> proposal and delivery handoff -> support and cost review. Do not spend the first month expanding the gallery, generating more brand media or building a generalized client portal.

Keep billing and accounting in an appropriate existing system until a real integration need is known. Keep a knowledge register with source, owner, last verification date and permitted use. Use normal database rules for money and permissions. Introduce client tenancy only after explicit isolation, export, deletion and access tests.

Every reusable workflow should eventually contain its process map, configuration, allowed actions, example inputs, test set, failure procedure, time estimate and evidence of outcomes. That package is more defensible than the number of agents displayed on a website.

## 10 Acquire evidence before scaling acquisition

Start from zero assumed warm prospects. Past paid work may provide experience or permissioned examples; the plan does not assume referrals, testimonials or client logos can be used.

**First 30-day experiment:** research 15 property agencies and 15 commercial cleaning/maintenance firms. Verify that each is operating, has a relevant channel and recurring workflow, and identify an appropriate public business contact. These are research targets; no actual list or outreach campaign has been executed here.

Seek 10 discovery conversations, then three proposals addressing observed problems and one paid pilot. This is a management target, not an assumed funnel conversion. Track researched -> contacted -> replied -> interviewed -> qualified -> proposed -> deposit paid. Capture dates, sources and loss reasons. Never count a demonstration or a friendly response as a sale.

**Discovery script:**

- Walk me through the last enquiry or job that became difficult. What happened next?
- How many similar cases occurred in the last month, and where are those records?
- Which steps require someone to chase information or re-enter it?
- How much time did those steps take? What errors or delays can we observe?
- Who owns the process, supplies approved information and signs off a change?
- What have you already tried or paid for? What would justify buying this now?
- Can we establish a baseline and test one narrow change with a fixed budget?

**Suggested conversation opener:** "I am researching how service businesses handle enquiries and move them into quotes or bookings. I would like to understand the last time that process became slow or messy. If there is a useful fit, I can propose a small, measured workflow project." This is a draft for the founder, not a message sent to anyone.

Choose the niche using paid commitment, data readiness, measurable value, delivery effort and reuse. After five interviews in a cohort, revise the problem statement if no repeated pain emerges. After three comparable proposals with no deposits, inspect fit, trust, value and scope before changing price. Small samples are directional; they do not establish market conversion rates.

Growth channels after proof: permissioned case study -> founder explanation of the workflow -> direct business introductions -> complementary website/IT/accounting partners. Paid advertising becomes a test only after the offer converts and delivery capacity exists. A successful internal process can demonstrate workmanship, but is not a substitute for independent client results.

**Weekly operating review:** pipeline next actions; cash collected and unpaid invoices; delivery hours versus estimate; failing workflows and unresolved incidents; actual provider spend; client outcomes; one decision and its evidence. AI can prepare the review, but the founder owns its conclusions.

**Spend discipline:** no budget was supplied, so this report does not allocate cash as if approved. Use existing tools first, client-owned usage accounts, and deposits before significant delivery costs. Set a specific discretionary monthly cap before new subscriptions. Account separately for mandatory registration, professional advice, connectivity, travel, equipment and founder living costs. These have not been priced or assumed free.

**Day-90 decision:** expand only if the workflow produces accepted outcomes, contribution remains acceptable after logged delivery time, and a buyer pays for ongoing care. If not, revise the segment, scope or service model. A one-off implementation business can be valid even when recurring care is not yet justified.

## 12 Growth potential with capacity constraints

The sensible sequence is one useful workflow -> repeat it for similar clients -> maintain it profitably -> add adjacent workflows -> productize common requirements. Ghana is the learning market; expansion to diaspora clients or other countries should follow demand and delivery readiness, not an unsupported continental growth projection.

**Illustrative first-year scenarios, not forecasts.** Fees exclude tax and separately billed external usage. Implementation revenue assumes the projects are delivered; recurring revenue counts active client-months, not year-end MRR multiplied by 12. Discovery revenue is excluded to avoid assuming extra sales or double-counting credits. Cash collection, churn and overhead could change the outcome materially.

| Scenario | Delivered implementations | Active care months | Year-end care clients | Annual fee revenue | Exit MRR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cautious | 4 x GHS 9,000 | 18 x GHS 2,500 | 3 | GHS 81,000 | GHS 7,500 |
| Working case | 8 x GHS 12,000 | 36 x GHS 3,000 | 6 | GHS 204,000 | GHS 18,000 |
| Expansion | 12 x GHS 18,000 | 60 x GHS 4,000 | 10 | GHS 456,000 | GHS 40,000 |

These client-month totals assume gradual acquisition, roughly six paid care months per eventual year-end care client and no churn in the illustration. Attach rates are high assumptions, not demonstrated probabilities. Actual start dates, cancellations and renewals must replace them.

**Capacity check:** the working case assumes 45 hours per implementation and eight support hours per active client-month: 8 x 45 + 36 x 8 = 648 delivery hours/year. At 25 hours/week for 46 weeks, with 35% reserved for non-delivery work, 747.5 hours remain. That leaves about 100 hours of buffer; peak monthly demand could still exceed capacity. At 15 hours/week, only 448.5 delivery hours remain, so this case does not fit.

The expansion case assumes 60 hours/build and 10 hours/client-month, requiring 1,320 hours. Even 40 hours/week over 46 weeks leaves only 1,196 delivery hours after the same reserve. It needs trained help, lower measured effort or slower growth. Revenue is not profit; include founder sales time and overhead before deciding whether to hire.

**Proposed expansion gates:** three similar paid deployments; clear buyer outcome; two actual care renewals; contribution target met with real hours; documented incident and handover procedures. Only then test another workflow, delivery partner or market. No numeric valuation, market share or funding outcome is implied.
