# Aksen Labs positioning and market strategy

Prepared for the Aksen Labs founder | 7 September 2026

Scope: global positioning patterns, African business context, current Aksen messaging and The Frame Shop proposal. The founder's direction is a full digital transformation agency, based in Ghana and open to clients beyond Ghana. Recommendations below are strategic judgments to test, not measured market outcomes.

## 01 The positioning decision

**Aksen Labs should be positioned as a digital transformation agency helping African businesses grow through technology.** Its work can span customer experience, commerce, business operations, data and new digital products. AI strengthens that work wherever it improves the result.

The founder's ambition has three distinct levels. The mission is African business prosperity. The agency helps a particular business improve how it earns revenue and operates. Individual solutions and future products address specific jobs. Keeping these levels clear gives Aksen room to grow while making each engagement understandable.

**Recommended company description:** Aksen Labs is a digital transformation agency helping African businesses grow, serve customers better and run more effectively. We design and build websites, business systems and digital products, connect the tools teams rely on, and apply AI where it makes the work more useful. We work with clients from understanding the problem through implementation, team adoption and improvement.

**Recommended public promise:** Technology that helps your business grow.

**Mission:** Help African businesses prosper through the effective use of technology.

**Geographic line:** Based in Ghana. Built for African ambition. Open to businesses wherever we can deliver useful results.

Use the mission in the company story and the public promise in the opening sales message. Explain prosperity through observable improvements: more profitable business, dependable service, stronger operations and better decisions. Avoid implying that technology guarantees these outcomes.

The scope is full digital transformation, delivered through defined engagements. A small website project can be a valid engagement; so can a connected commerce and operations system. Discovery should decide the appropriate scale. Every prospective client should be able to recognize a concrete starting point.

**What changes from earlier strategy:** retain Aksen Labs; replace workflow-only company positioning; keep Ghana as a base and practical acquisition focus; treat Nigerian and other qualified opportunities as normal business. Narrow campaigns remain useful for learning. They do not redefine the entire agency.

Evidence basis: founder direction, local source review and the global/African comparisons on the following pages. The wording is a recommendation. This research does not establish completed Aksen client transformations or a continent-wide operating footprint.

## 03 What the global comparisons show

Large consultancies and agencies commonly combine a broad business promise with focused services and proprietary products. This establishes a viable positioning pattern, not proof that copying their language will win Aksen customers.

| Business | Observed structure | Lesson for Aksen |
|---|---|---|
| Thoughtworks | Technology consultancy with AI/works, engineering, transformation pathways and managed services | A product can sit inside a broader capability story |
| Publicis Sapient | Enterprise AI solutions supported by experts and platforms such as Slingshot and Bodhi | Describe the business problems that connected technology addresses |
| BCG X | Design and technology delivery, customer experience, platforms and new ventures, with a product library | A parent brand can own transformation relationships and multiple products |
| DEPT | Growth-oriented offer across marketing, customer experience, commerce, technology/data and AI; Deptify is a named offering | Group breadth around business needs rather than a long list of tools |

Sources: [Thoughtworks homepage](https://www.thoughtworks.com/), [Publicis Sapient homepage](https://www.publicissapient.com/), [BCG X overview and product library](https://www.bcg.com/x/), [DEPT homepage](https://www.deptagency.com/). Publisher: each named company. Undated live pages, checked 7 September 2026. Descriptions reflect published offers, not independently tested performance.

Some current global homepages lead heavily with AI. That is a useful counterpoint: there is no universal rule that a broad agency must hide the technology. Aksen's problem is that the current AI framing makes its wider offer hard to discover. Its young brand needs more explicit explanation than a widely known consultancy.

McKinsey's August 2026 survey reports that 80% of respondents experienced individual productivity improvement from AI, while 37% attributed some enterprise earnings impact to it. Higher performers more often reported workflow redesign and growth ambitions. The survey covered 1,719 respondents in 97 nations, with fieldwork in May-June 2026. These are self-reported associations, not causal proof or a Ghanaian small-business sample.

Source: [McKinsey Global Survey, The state of AI in 2026](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai), 25 August 2026; checked 7 September 2026.

**Implication:** Aksen's value should include business diagnosis, implementation, adoption and measurement. Making software easier to build increases the importance of choosing and implementing the right change. AI can contribute to growth and new offerings as well as administrative efficiency.

Custom building earns its place when the required business fit or integration justifies it. This is a delivery recommendation, not a procurement endorsement of any researched vendor.

## 05 Bringing the research into Ghana and Nigeria

The local opportunity is to help businesses make productive use of technology. IFC's 2024 research describes incomplete digitalization: access to tools does not necessarily become intensive use across business functions. This supports an offer that includes implementation and adoption. It does not establish how many firms can afford Aksen's services.

Source: [IFC, Digital Opportunities in African Businesses](https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2024/digital-opportunities-in-african-businesses), 2024, and [IFC release](https://www.ifc.org/en/pressroom/2024/ifc-report-shows-digitalization-holds-immense-promise-economic-potential-for-african-businesses-of-all-sizes), 16 May 2024; checked 7 September 2026. Regional evidence, not a 2026 country-specific market census.

IFC's 2023 analysis covered 3,325 microenterprises across seven countries, including Ghana and Nigeria. Costs, skills and perceived lack of need were barriers to adoption. Its authors distinguish performance associations from causation. These pooled findings should inform discovery questions, not assumptions about every established business.

Source: [IFC, Digital Technologies Are a Useful Yet Underutilized Tool for African Microenterprises](https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2023/digital-technologies-in-africa), 2023; checked 7 September 2026. Underlying fieldwork predates publication; many sampled firms were very small and informal.

Ghana-specific World Bank analysis recommends foundational technology adoption and skills as the base for more advanced AI and automation. Its 2021 publication is historical structural evidence, not a current readiness estimate. For Aksen, the practical lesson is to assess records, responsibilities and staff use before promising AI improvements.

Source: [World Bank, Technological transformation of Ghanaian MSMEs](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2021/11/24/the-key-to-creating-more-jobs-in-ghana-driving-technological-transformation-of-micro-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises), 24 November 2021; checked 7 September 2026.

**Recommended market approach:** operate from Ghana, pursue accessible Ghanaian prospects and qualified Nigerian opportunities such as TFS, then expand based on evidence and delivery capacity. A country is neither an automatic qualification nor a disqualification.

Localize each engagement's currency, payment arrangements, buying process, customer channels, delivery commitments, support hours and third-party dependencies. Establish these from the actual business. The Nigerian proposal is useful evidence that Aksen already contemplates cross-border work; it does not establish a Nigerian operating footprint or a validated market segment.

No current Ghana or Nigeria willingness-to-pay estimate was established. Initial segment selection should therefore follow reachable buyers with a costly problem, an accountable owner, usable information and a budget compatible with delivery.

## 07 Make the full offer easy to buy

Organize services into four areas a business owner can recognize. Treat strategy, implementation and team adoption as the method across all four. AI can contribute within each area; it does not need to become a separate compulsory purchase.

| Service area | Client-facing explanation | Examples within a defined scope |
|---|---|---|
| Customer experience and commerce | Help customers discover, choose and buy from you | Websites, shops, booking, payments, enquiry handling and customer support |
| Business systems and operations | Connect the information and work your team relies on | Customer records, admin tools, approvals, integrations and workflow automation |
| Data and business insight | Make performance easier to understand and act on | Data cleanup, reporting, dashboards and decision support |
| Digital products and new services | Turn a business idea into something people can use | Product discovery, prototypes, portals and custom applications |

These are capability groups, not promises that every technology or industry is supported. Aksen should describe the part it can deliver, identify any partners needed and agree boundaries for each engagement. Broader organizational restructuring or specialist enterprise migrations require their own expertise and scope.

**A practical engagement sequence:** understand the business and its goal; prioritize the opportunity; design and build or configure the solution; help the team adopt it; review performance and improve where there is continuing value.

Offer a focused implementation when the need is already clear. Use a discovery engagement when the business needs diagnosis or a roadmap. Offer continuing support or improvement when there is a defined ongoing responsibility. A software subscription pays for access; a managed service pays for operating work. Explain them separately even when sold together.

Avoid turning "full transformation" into a vague all-inclusive package. For TFS, an agreed commerce and operations scope can form a coherent first engagement. Another business may begin with a booking system or a reporting problem. Aksen earns a wider relationship through delivery.

**AI as a multiplier in plain language:** AI helps people use information, prepare work and serve customers with less repeated effort. Its contribution depends on accurate records, reliable systems and clear business responsibilities. Payment confirmation, calculations and permissions should follow verified data and explicit rules. Commercial exceptions remain with the responsible person.

This service structure and delivery method are recommendations derived from the research and TFS scope. No new delivery capacity, service guarantee or price is asserted.

## 08 Build products under Aksen Labs

The parent company owns the mission, trust and client relationship. A product should own a defined job for a defined customer. This allows several products over time while keeping Aksen Labs understandable.

| Layer | What it means | How to describe it |
|---|---|---|
| Aksen Labs | The digital transformation agency | Business outcomes, capabilities, approach and evidence |
| Client engagement | A scoped implementation for a particular business | The Frame Shop commerce and operations proposal |
| Internal reusable component | Code, templates or tools that help Aksen deliver | Internal capability; no standalone sales claim |
| Named product | A repeatable offering a customer can buy and use | A specific product name followed by by Aksen Labs |

Candidate product themes include commerce operations for a repeated retail need, a customer-service workspace or industry-specific intake. These are hypotheses, not launch announcements. Existing admin screens, agent demonstrations and client code do not automatically establish a standalone product.

**Recommended product gate:** test whether several independent customers share substantially the same problem and core workflow; establish willingness to pay; measure setup and support effort; define onboarding and ownership; verify a reliable product journey. Three comparable paid implementations can be a useful internal learning target, not a universal threshold or market proof.

Check the case for configuring existing software before building a competing product. Aksen's advantage may lie in industry fit, connected workflows, adoption support or local operating knowledge. A larger feature list by itself is a weak reason to build.

For the website now, service navigation should carry the main agency story. Place agent examples under relevant services or demonstrations. Add a Products destination when there is a real offering with a clear buyer, scope and next step. If showing experiments earlier, explicitly call them experiments.

Arkesel demonstrates the product naming pattern, and CWG/Fifthlab demonstrates services alongside software. Aksen should borrow that clarity while choosing its own product scope from client evidence. No partnership, resale authorization or technical integration with those businesses is implied.

## 09 Recommended messaging and website journey

**Hero descriptor:** Digital transformation for African businesses

**Hero headline:** Technology that helps your business grow.

**Hero supporting copy:** Aksen Labs helps you improve how your business sells, serves customers and gets work done. We build websites, business systems and digital products, connect your tools, and use AI to help your team do more.

**Primary action:** Discuss your business

**Secondary action:** Explore our services

**Short company bio:** Aksen Labs is a Ghana-based digital transformation agency helping African businesses grow through technology. We bring together digital experiences, business systems and practical AI, from strategy and implementation to team adoption.

**Founder explanation:** We look at what a business is trying to achieve and where its current setup is getting in the way. Then we design and implement the right digital solution. That could connect its website, payments and customer records, improve its internal operations, or become a new digital product. AI helps us make those systems and the people using them more effective.

The opening headline should stay stable as visitors explore examples. Use the next section to show business journeys: retail discovery through payment and fulfilment; a service enquiry through quotation and delivery; management records through decisions. This gives visitors a picture of the whole engagement.

**Recommended page sequence:** company promise and category; recognizable business opportunities; four service areas; approach; honest project examples; the role of AI; about Aksen and its African mission; contact. Products can join the sequence when available.

**Recommended navigation:** Services, Approach, Work, About, Insights. Use Work only with available material clearly labeled by status; until then, Examples is more precise. Add Products when there is something independently purchasable. Navigation items need working destinations before release.

The contact journey should accept both problems and ambitions. Start with "What would you like your business to do better?" Offer choices covering online sales, customer service, internal operations, business visibility and a new digital product, with room for an uncertain or unlisted need. Capture country for scoping without using it to exclude prospects automatically.

This is ready-to-use draft copy. It needs buyer comprehension testing and a coordinated implementation across pages, metadata, guide prompts and fallbacks. It has not been deployed by this research task.

## 10 What to do next and what remains uncertain

**First, adopt the positioning source.** Use the accompanying messaging document as the current reference for Aksen's identity and geography. Keep past strategy files as history and apply their useful delivery discipline without retaining the superseded workflow-only category.

**Next, update the complete public journey.** Implement the hero and metadata together with navigation, service descriptions, opportunity intake and the Aksen Guide. A broad headline leading into an agent-only recommendation flow would preserve the existing confusion. Use the file-specific change map in the messaging document.

**Then, validate with actual buyers.** As a practical first test, speak with five reachable Ghanaian business owners and five Nigerian owners, including TFS only if appropriate and welcomed. This is a proposed qualitative sample, not a statistically representative survey. Show the message briefly, ask what Aksen does and what they would approach it for, and record their own words before explaining.

| Question to resolve | Evidence to collect | Decision it informs |
|---|---|---|
| Do buyers understand the category? | Unaided descriptions of Aksen | Whether the supporting copy is specific enough |
| Which problems create action? | Recent examples, current costs and urgency | The next focused offer or campaign |
| Can Aksen deliver profitably? | Scoping, build, rework and support hours | Scope, price and concurrent workload |
| Is a pattern reusable? | Similar needs across independent paid projects | Service template or product investment |
| Does a market justify more attention? | Qualified conversations, paid conversion and delivery friction | Where to concentrate acquisition |

Do not require Ghana to be "finished" before accepting a viable Nigerian project. Equally, do not scatter prospecting effort across many countries without learning. Choose a manageable set of opportunities, record why they qualify and review what their economics and delivery teach you.

Research limitations: vendor websites describe positioning and advertised features; none of their outcomes was independently audited here. Global AI survey findings are observational and not Ghana/Nigeria SME forecasts. Local structural studies are older and do not establish present willingness to pay. TFS files do not confirm commercial acceptance or delivery. Aksen capacity, margins and recurring product demand remain to be measured.

The research stopped after global and African structures converged, the Arkesel identity was verified and the important local contradictions were bounded. More generic competitor searches are unlikely to settle buyer comprehension or demand. Those questions now require client conversations and delivery evidence.
