- Kova naming: resolved as KOVA IQ through original Arkesel page, with VoiceConnect cross-reference.

- TFS prices and case-study status: conflicting historical pricing explicitly flagged, no terms changed, no completed-result claims.
- AI productivity: 2026 McKinsey canonical page verified, avoiding older indexed survey headlines. Survey associations do not establish causal impact.
- African readiness: access/use denominators vary; omit headline percentages rather than conflate formal firms, microenterprises and populations.
- Nigeria current SME demand: no fresh representative willingness-to-pay evidence established; pursue buyer discovery, not a fabricated market-size estimate.
- Product readiness: agency platform and internal reusable tools do not establish independently purchasable products; product gate is a recommendation.
